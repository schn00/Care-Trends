Care Trends

A tool for caregivers to track health information for someone in their care.
Privacy Statement
Care Trends does not run any server and does not collect your data. Everything you enter is stored in your own Google account's Google Drive. The creator of Care Trends never receives, stores, or can access your information.

Where your data is stored

All information you enter is stored in your own Google account's Google Drive. It is saved in a file that this app creates in your Drive (named care-trends-data.json), and it stays under your control. A copy is also kept in your device's browser so the app loads instantly and works offline.

What the app's creator can see

Nothing. The creator of Care Trends does not receive, store, or have any ability to access your information. Your data never passes through any server run by the creator.

What the app accesses

When you connect Google Drive, Care Trends asks only for permission to create and update its own data file in your Drive. It cannot see, read, or change any other files in your Drive.

Your control

You can view your data anytime in your Google Drive, export it to an Excel file from within the app, or delete it. You can revoke the app's access to your Drive at any time in your Google Account security settings, under “Third-party apps & services.”

Security of your account

Because your data lives in your Google account, keeping that account secure — your password and sign-in settings — is your responsibility. If several caregivers share one Google account to view the same log, all of them are responsible for keeping that account and its sign-in details secure.

This is not medical software

Care Trends is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not replace professional medical care. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical decisions.
