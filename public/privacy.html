Care Trends — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8/27/26

Your records stay on your device, and in your own Google Drive if you turn on sync. They are never sent to me and I cannot read them. Care Trends is a personal, non-commercial project with no company behind it.

Your data

Everything you enter is saved in your browser on the device you are using. If you connect Google Drive, the same records are also saved as a single file, care-trends-data.json, in your own Drive.

The app asks only for the drive.file permission — the narrowest Drive permission Google offers. It grants access solely to the file the app creates, and cannot see anything else in your Drive.

The sign-in service

Signing in to Google uses a credential that cannot safely be published in a web page, so sign-in requests go through a small function I operate at /api/token. It exchanges sign-in codes for access tokens, renews expired ones, and stores your renewal token in an encrypted cookie in your browser that page scripts cannot read.

It keeps no database and writes no records. Your care data never passes through it — those records travel directly between your browser and Google.

Being straight about the trade-off: the access tokens crossing this function could read and write your Care Trends file. I do not retain or use them, but you cannot inspect that code from your browser the way you can inspect the app itself. The source is public at https://github.com/schn00/Care-Trends.

What I collect

Nothing. No analytics, no advertising, no trackers, no accounts. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, handles ordinary request information such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service under its own privacy policy. I do not access it.

Google

Care Trends' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. That data is used only to provide sync. It is never transferred to others, used for advertising, sold, or used to train any model.

Removing your data

  • Disconnect this device in the app revokes access and deletes the sign-in cookie.
  • Delete care-trends-data.json from your Drive, then empty the trash.
  • Clear the site's data in your browser, or delete the home-screen app.
  • Revoke access any time at your Google permissions page.

I hold nothing, so there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf.

Not medical software

Care Trends is a record-keeping tool, not a medical device. It gives no medical advice and is no substitute for professional judgement. Do not rely on it in an emergency, and verify anything that matters against the original prescription or clinical instruction.

Availability

This is a personal project with no guarantee of uptime or continued existence. If the sign-in service stops, each device keeps working with the data already on it and your Drive file stays in your Drive. Export a backup of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how information is handled, the date above will change and the revision will show in the public repository.
